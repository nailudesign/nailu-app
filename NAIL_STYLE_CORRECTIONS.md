# Corrected Nail Style Definitions

## Glass French Nails
**Correct Definition:**
- French manicure style with tips
- **Edge/Tips**: Silver, shiny, glass-like reflective finish
- **Base**: Can be any color (nude, pink, clear, etc.)
- **Key Feature**: The tips look like shiny glass or mirror - very reflective and metallic silver

**Example Prompts:**
```
Glass French manicure with sheer nude base and silver mirror-like reflective tips that shine like glass, metallic chrome finish on nail edges, ultra glossy and reflective, sophisticated French style
```

```
Glass French nails with transparent pink base and shiny silver chrome tips with glass-like reflection, metallic mirror finish, elegant and modern
```

---

## Nuance Nails
**Correct Definition:**
- Multiple **soft, muted colors** mixed together on nails
- Colors are **not strong** - they are subtle, pastel, or desaturated
- **Blended/mixed** effect - colors flow into each other
- Often looks like watercolor, marble, or abstract art
- **Soft aesthetic** - gentle, understated, artistic

**Example Prompts:**
```
Nuance nail design with soft muted colors blending together - pale beige, dusty pink, light gray mixed in watercolor style, gentle pastel tones, not strong colors, artistic and understated
```

```
Nuance nails with multiple soft colors - cream, mauve, sage green subtly mixed together in marble effect, muted pastel palette, gentle blending, no strong colors
```
